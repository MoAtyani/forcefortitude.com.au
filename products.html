External Pen Tester

Fortive Scanner

Fortive Scanner is our AI-enhanced external attack surface management and penetration testing platform. It continuously discovers, enumerates, and probes your internet-facing infrastructure — the same way real attackers do.

Unlike traditional scanners that rely only on CVE databases, Fortive Scanner uses machine learning to correlate findings, predict exploitation likelihood, and prioritise what actually matters to your business — cutting alert noise by up to 70%.

Internal Pen Tester

Fortive Network

Fortive Network is our internal penetration testing and lateral movement simulation platform. Deploy it inside your network to simulate post-breach adversary behaviour — discovering attack paths that external tools simply cannot see.

Built around real adversary TTPs (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures) from the MITRE ATT&CK framework, Fortive Network maps the exact paths an attacker would take from initial access to domain dominance.
