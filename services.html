Digital Forensics & Legal Advisory

Digital evidence can be complex, particularly when significant technical information is condensed into police reports and investigator statements. Understanding what the underlying evidence actually demonstrates — and equally, what it does not demonstrate — can be critical when assessing a legal matter.

Our service provides lawyers and legal counsel with independent technical analysis, practical advice, and rigorous evidentiary scrutiny to ensure your defense or case strategy is grounded in verified digital facts.

Independent Technical Scrutiny

Our objective is not to advocate for either party, but to provide independent, technically informed scrutiny of digital evidence. We help legal teams better understand the evidence and determine whether the conclusions being relied upon are properly supported — especially where technical methodology, extraction process, interpretation, or limitations are not immediately apparent from the documentation provided.

Services Provided to Legal Counsel

Examination and review of police reports, forensic reports, and statements involving digital evidence
Translation of complex forensic findings into clear, legally relevant explanations
Assessment of whether conclusions presented in reports are supported by underlying digital evidence
Identification of inconsistencies, technical limitations, assumptions, and evidential gaps
Review of digital forensic acquisition and examination methodologies
Analysis of mobile phone, computer, cloud, communication, metadata, location, and other digital evidence
Identification of alternative explanations or interpretations of digital artefacts
Development of technically informed questions for investigators and forensic examiners
Assistance in identifying areas requiring further examination, disclosure, or independent testing
Independent technical advice to assist counsel in assessing the strength and limitations of digital evidence
