About Us

We are Force Fortitude

A 100% Australian-owned cybersecurity company on a mission to empower businesses with military-grade, AI-powered cyber defence.

Our Story

Built From the Attacker's Perspective

Force Fortitude was founded by offensive security professionals who spent years understanding how cyber adversaries operate. That hands-on experience in ethical hacking, red teaming, and threat research became the foundation of everything we build.

We recognised a gap in the market: businesses needed more than compliance checklists. They needed a security partner that could think, adapt, and respond like a real attacker — one armed with modern AI capabilities to outpace the speed of today's threats.

Today, Force Fortitude operates from New South Wales, serving Australian organisations across industries with a combination of expert-led services and our proprietary Fortive Suite of AI-powered penetration testing tools.
